## Missed points

- §PnR: the glossary's own definition of placement and routing, including
  that each of the two steps ends with a legality check of its result. The
  listed pages use the entry only for the scope disagreement with S2.
- §GDSII: shuttle providers broadly ask for the data to be converted to
  OASIS. The pages say OASIS replaced GDSII for tape-out and that KLayout can
  convert, but not that the conversion is what providers request.
- §metrics: metrics are quantitative measures of a design's physical
  characteristics (area, worst clock slack, average voltage drop), widened in
  LibreLane to any information generated at run time. No S4-cited definition
  exists although other pages use the word.
- §SPICE / §SPICE netlist: SPICE simulates analog circuits under physical
  conditions (temperature, extracted parasitics, transistor switching speed)
  that most Verilog simulators do not cover, and SPICE itself is not part of
  any included LibreLane flow although SPICE netlists are.
- §SDC / §Tcl: SDC is a Tcl-based format supporting a subset of the timing
  tools' Tcl commands, and Yosys, OpenROAD, and Magic are all
  Tcl-programmable. The S4 statement on the SDC page keeps only the
  "timing, power, and area" part.
