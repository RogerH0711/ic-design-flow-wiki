## Missed points

- Expression coverage: a further form with its own section (§Expression Coverage), recording which Boolean term combinations of each expression occurred; absent from every listed page.
- FSM coverage is described as experimental and deliberately narrow: only a conservative subset of clocked state machines is recognised (§FSM Coverage).
- Toggle coverage has stated exclusions and compression: local variables in tasks or begin/end blocks, signals starting with an underscore, integers and very wide signals are not covered, and counts are summed over all instances of a module with the same parameters (§Toggle Coverage).
- Coverage can be switched off for parts of the code, and lines and branches containing `$stop` are excluded automatically because they are assumed to be error checks that should not occur (§Suppressing Coverage).
- Speed options trade away checking: the fast X-assignment/initialisation options may raise the risk of reset bugs, and disabling assertions is faster but may hide design errors (§Benchmarking & Optimization).
