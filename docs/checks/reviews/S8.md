## Missed points

- Scope of the process: a new design goes through a proposal, whereas a minor modification to an existing design is handled as a pull request or an issue attached to that design (§Concept and RFC).
- The skeleton method is explicitly not mandatory; its stated benefit is that the basics come in first with one review and the custom details follow over time (§Initial Skeleton Design).
- The register definition is written in Hjson and the top-level module of the block instantiates a register model that is generated automatically from it (§Initial Skeleton Design). The wiki mentions the register definition but not that the register model is auto-generated.
- As the design develops in the repository it passes through named design stages "D0", "D1", and so on before it is plugged into the top level (§Full Design).
- Top-level inclusion has two further conditions the wiki omits: the block must be of acceptable quality, and the change is made through a pull request (§Top Level Inclusion). Related: an RTL interface change such as changed interrupts also requires work outside the RTL, in the testbench, the regenerated top level, and software (§Change of Interrupts).
